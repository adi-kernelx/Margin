Label guide
1 · Definition
Introduces the meaning of a concept, including prose such as “is called” or “we say”.
2 · Theorem
A mathematical result: theorem, lemma, proposition, corollary or claim.
3 · Proof
An argument establishing a result.
4 · Example
A worked example, exercise or problem statement.
5 · Notation
Introduces symbols or conventions without primarily defining a mathematical concept.
6 · Algorithm
A procedure or sequence of computational steps.
7 · Other
Background, discussion, references or other text outside the six roles.

If the block mixes roles or extraction makes it unreadable, skip it for review rather than guessing. Use the PDF's physical page for context. Export a backup after each session.
